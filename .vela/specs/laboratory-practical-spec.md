# Laboratory practical extension
Version 1.0.0

Map each activity to a current supervised practical objective. Keep observation, calculation and interpretation distinct. Record instrument range, resolution, units, uncertainty, controls and relevant assumptions. Any virtual readings are labelled simulated or synthetic. Independently verify worked calculations, uncertainty propagation where assessed, plots and acceptance tolerances.

Use reviewed apparatus diagrams and accessible data tables. Assess recognition of a reading, a procedure for analysing data, error correction and transfer to new measurements. A model must not imply that replaying a simulation satisfies practical attendance or competency. Physical procedures and safety instructions must come from the approved supervised manual and be reviewed by the institution. Never turn an unsupervised simulation into a claim of laboratory certification.
