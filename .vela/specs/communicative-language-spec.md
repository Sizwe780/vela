# Communicative-language extension
Version 1.0.0

Register a separate profile and reviewed source map for each language. Match lessons to actual communicative situations and the current module outcomes. Teach meaning in context, language-appropriate register and a transparent progression from recognition through guided production to a changed scenario. Keep transcripts, subtitles and pronunciation or signing notes accessible without replacing the target-language representation.

Require a qualified reviewer for the language variety and media. Represent accepted variants explicitly, and keep automatic pronunciation or signing feedback provisional until validated. For SASL, use reviewed video with appropriate facial expression, body orientation and signing space. English captions and gloss support access while the signed representation remains the target. Do not transfer speech-only features into signed-language modules.
