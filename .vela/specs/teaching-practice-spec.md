Map each task to the current school-placement guide and mentor/assessor role. Use fictional or properly anonymised cases to teach how to distinguish observation from inference, formulate evidence-based reflection and choose an appropriate follow-up. Explain rubric categories and use more than one plausible response when the evidence warrants it.

Keep practice feedback, mentor verification, attendance and institutional marks separate. Do not request identifiable pupil details, school records, recordings or pictures in course examples, shared surfaces or tutor prompts. Any actual placement evidence needs an approved storage, retention and authorisation process before the feature is enabled.

## Guided observation and portfolio rehearsal

- Begin with a focused observation question. Record observable behaviour, a bounded interpretation, a plausible alternative and a next observation that could distinguish the interpretations.
- Use synthetic cases for observation, planning, assessment, feedback and post-observation reflection. Label that evidence as simulated. Reuse BPT1501 case, rubric and reflection components where they help.
- Pair a blank practice sheet with a worked sample. Use a rubric that rewards evidence specificity, warranted interpretation and feasible follow-up.
- Keep identifiable placement material outside course storage and embedding inputs. Use fictional evidence cards in tutor prompts, notes demos and exercises. Actual sign-off, attendance and the official portfolio belong to institutional channels.
- Convert historical tasks into competence areas. Keep official forms, dated logistics, signatures and submission instructions in the editorial source crosswalk. Confirm the current authoritative pack and usage permissions before publishing institution-specific guidance.
