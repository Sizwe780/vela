# Education/PCK domain extension
Version: 1.1.0

Connect disciplinary knowledge to how learners develop it. Each unit includes a PCK teaching note, a likely learner misconception with a diagnostic explanation, a representation or teaching move, and a formative check with an anticipated response and feedback. Account for language, prior knowledge, access needs and multiple representations. Teacher-training transfer tasks require an instructional decision and a rationale, not just a repeated subject calculation.

When authoritative CAPS evidence is supplied, identify the phase/grade, subject, document edition/page and verified learning requirement. Without that evidence, label alignment as a teaching suggestion and ask for the relevant phase/grade when needed. Never invent CAPS quotations, mandated lesson sequences or assessment allocations. Qualification SAQA metadata alone does not establish CAPS alignment. Learner scenarios must be fictional or anonymized and must not disclose personal records.

PCK complements subject correctness: a helpful analogy must state where it stops working. Common mistakes are useful formative evidence, not labels of student ability. School-based examples require ethical handling of pupil information and appropriate institution-specific review.

## Independent teacher reasoning
Explain a teaching principle visibly before asking the student teacher to make an instructional choice. Present fictional classroom scenarios question-first and gate proposed responses and rationales behind Show answer. A suggested response is not a universal solution or verified CAPS policy. Revealing it cannot award mastery.

Place a visual at the point where learners need to compare choices, follow a process or relate a framework to a decision. Give the visual a prompt, descriptive labels, a keyboard path and a text explanation. Name the applicable framework and its source. Keep framework descriptions distinct from professional duties and institution-specific rules. Use fictional or anonymised cases, and verify the rights and captions for any classroom photographs or portfolio artefacts before registration.
