# Universal mastery specification
Version: 1.1.0

Recognition identifies a concept and explains why it applies. Procedure executes justified steps. Error correction diagnoses the cause of a plausible error and repairs it. Transfer selects and adapts an idea in a changed context, with assumptions and limits stated.

Every unit provides answered opportunities for all four stages. Assessment prompts must be distinct, with answer keys or worked solutions and diagnostic rationale. Completion, confidence/self-rating and demonstrated correctness are separate evidence. Reading completion cannot award assessed mastery. Recall ratings remain labelled self-assessment and update review scheduling. Quiz evaluation must preserve learner isolation and transactional/idempotent writes. A skill tree displays evidence with its type and date; it is not an institutional grade or SAQA achievement certificate.

## Answer reveal is not evidence
Show answer, drawer opening, session navigation and reading completion must not be recorded as correct answers or earned mastery. Learners attempt independently before inspecting feedback. Recognition, procedure, error correction and transfer need their own assessed or explicitly self-rated evidence; a model explanation is not proof of understanding. Session switching resets exercise reveal state and active tutor context.
