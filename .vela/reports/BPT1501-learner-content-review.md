## Site-wide use

Run `npm run audit:visuals` against the intended database. It reports current session coverage and the IDs of lessons without a registered visual or accepted figure. Its checks detect malformed directives, unavailable module tools and unregistered lesson images. A visual still requires subject review for accuracy, relevance, accessibility and mobile readability.

Add visuals where they explain an idea or support a student decision. Count worked coordinate plots, diagrams, figures and interactive explorations by what they show. Use source provenance and a reuse basis for externally authored images. The lesson Markdown renderer only accepts a locally registered figure on its own line. Tutor and citation Markdown retain their existing image restrictions.

The staging baseline found a usable visual in all 5 BPT1501 sessions, 16 of 31 CHE1501 sessions, 12 of 78 MAT1503 sessions and 6 of 30 MAT1512 sessions. The coverage scan reported zero invalid figures or directives in those four modules. Prioritise science diagrams where spatial models matter and coordinate plots where students must connect algebra to geometry. Validate each model against the exact lesson example before changing the live lesson.
