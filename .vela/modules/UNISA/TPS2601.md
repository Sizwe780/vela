# TPS2601 · Teaching Practice for Senior Phase I

UNISA assigns TPS2601 to the PSM first level and classifies the module at NQF Level 6. Its purpose is guided observation and reflection during a school placement. Confirm dates, placement rules, relevant methodology and the evidence required with UNISA. [UNISA module catalogue](https://unisa.ac.za/sites/corporate/default/Register-to-study-through-Unisa/Subjects-%26-modules/All-modules/Teaching-practice-I-%E2%80%93-TPS2601) · [UNISA Teaching Practice Office](https://www.unisa.ac.za/sites/corporate/default/Colleges/Education/Schools%2C-departments%2C-centres-%26-instututes/Centres/Teaching-Practice-Office/Teaching-Practice-Modules)

Develop observation prompts that separate what was witnessed, interpretation and a possible next action. Use fictional or properly anonymised examples. Prioritise clear rubrics, reflective feedback and authorised mentor guidance. Keep placement evidence access-controlled and avoid adding identifiable learner records or photos to the tutor or searchable course material. Follow the [school-placement extension](../../specs/teaching-practice-spec.md).

The catalogue is registered. Vela can support preparation and private practice; UNISA’s placement, attendance and sign-off remain the formal record.
