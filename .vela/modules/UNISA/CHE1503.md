# CHE1503 · General Chemistry I (Practical)

UNISA lists a year module at NQF 5. The PSM stream lists CHE1501 and CHE1502 as co-requisites. It covers practical laboratory skills associated with chemical reactions, titrimetric analysis, physical chemistry, elementary organic work and laboratory safety. [UNISA module catalogue](https://unisa.ac.za/sites/corporate/default/Register-to-study-through-Unisa/Subjects-%26-modules/All-modules/Chemistry-I-%28Practical%29-%E2%80%93-CHE1503)

Build preparation around reading instruments, recording observations with units, choosing an analysis, checking uncertainty and interpreting results. Use fictional trial readings and guided predictions. A titration model must identify what it calculates and the assumptions behind it. Confirm every experiment, reagent and assessment format against the course’s current supervised practical manual. Follow the [laboratory-practical extension](../../specs/laboratory-practical-spec.md).

Vela exercises can rehearse analysis and documentation. Assessment of practical competency and attendance remains with the institution. The catalogue is registered and content is pending the current practical manual and subject review.
