# CHE1502 · General Chemistry IB

UNISA lists an NQF 5 year module covering bonding and introductory organic chemistry. CHE1501 is the 90104–PSM stream co-requisite. The public outcomes include physical properties, introductory stereochemistry, hydrocarbons, alkyl halides, alcohols, ethers, amines, and the principal carbonyl families. [UNISA module catalogue](https://unisa.ac.za/sites/corporate/default/Register-to-study-through-Unisa/Subjects-%26-modules/All-modules/General-Chemistry-IB-%E2%80%93-CHE1502)

## Build sequence to confirm with the current guide

1. Connect bonding and hybridisation to structural representations and functional-group nomenclature.
2. Distinguish constitutional isomers, conformations and introductory stereochemistry.
3. Use reaction conditions to reason about alkane, alkene and alkyne reactions.
4. Connect functional-group properties and reactions across derivatives, alcohols and the assigned carbonyl families. Confirm mechanism depth and course order before authoring.

Assessed scope excludes the standalone kinetics, aqueous acid–base equilibrium and thermochemistry topics discussed for CHE1501. Organic proton-transfer reasoning can appear where the current guide calls for it. Keep preparation grounded in the [organic chemistry extension](../../specs/organic-chemistry-content-spec.md).
