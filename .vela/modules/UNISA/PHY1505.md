## 2. Mandatory Mathematical Derivations Required by Syllabus

To satisfy Tutorial Letter 101/0/2022 outcomes, lessons cannot merely provide formulas; they must guide students through rigorous derivations:
1. **Kinematic Equations**: $\frac{dv}{dt} = a \implies v(t) = v_0 + at$, and $\frac{dx}{dt} = v_0 + at \implies x(t) = x_0 + v_0 t + \frac{1}{2}at^2$, leading to $v^2 = v_0^2 + 2a\Delta x$.
2. **Moments of Inertia ($I = \int r^2 dm$)**:
   - Thin rod about center ($I = \frac{1}{12}ML^2$) and about end ($I = \frac{1}{3}ML^2$)
   - Circular disc / cylinder about central axis ($I = \frac{1}{2}MR^2$)
   - Thin rectangular plate of sides $a$ and $b$ ($I = \frac{1}{12}M(a^2 + b^2)$)
   - Solid sphere of uniform density ($I = \frac{2}{5}MR^2$)
   - Spherical shell ($I = \frac{2}{3}MR^2$)
3. **Fluid Mechanics**:
   - Equation of Continuity from mass conservation: $dm_1 = dm_2 \implies \rho A_1 v_1 \Delta t = \rho A_2 v_2 \Delta t \implies A_1 v_1 = A_2 v_2$.
   - Bernoulli's equation from work-energy theorem on fluid volume element.

## 3. Visuals, Simulations & Interactive Lab Tools Needed

To ensure deep conceptual transfer and compete with premier edtech platforms, PHY1505 requires specific visual tools:

1. **Interactive Motion Grapher (`motion-grapher`)**:
   - Tri-coordinate synchronized plots: Position $x(t)$, Velocity $v(t)$, and Acceleration $a(t)$.
   - Draggable tangents to show instantaneous slope ($\frac{dx}{dt} = v$) and shaded regions showing integral displacement ($\int v dt = \Delta x$).
2. **Interactive Free-Body Diagram Canvas (`fbd-sandbox`)**:
   - Isolate a physical body (block on incline, elevator, coupled masses).
   - Drag and drop vector arrows representing distinct forces ($\mathbf{N}, \mathbf{f}_s, \mathbf{f}_k, \mathbf{F}_g, \mathbf{T}$).
   - Immediate feedback on equilibrium ($\sum \mathbf{F} = 0$) or non-equilibrium acceleration direction ($\sum \mathbf{F} = m\mathbf{a}$).
3. **Projectile Motion Trajectory Canvas (`projectile-sandbox`)**:
   - Sliders for initial velocity $v_0$, launch angle $\theta$, initial height $y_0$, and gravity $g$.
   - Real-time display of trajectory parabolic curve, maximum height, flight time, and velocity vectors ($v_x, v_y$).
4. **Rotational Moment of Inertia Explorer (`moment-of-inertia`)**:
   - 3D cutaway renderings of the 5 standard geometries (rod, disc, plate, cylinder, sphere).
   - Slice integration visualization showing thin cylindrical shells or strips adding up to the total mass moment.
5. **Fluid Flow & Venturi Tube Visualizer (`bernoulli-tube`)**:
   - Cross-section of a constricted pipe showing fluid streamlines, velocity vectors, and vertical manometer pressure tubes.
