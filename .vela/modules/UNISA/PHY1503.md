## 2. Interactive Virtual Lab Tools & Visualizations Needed

To provide world-class practical learning without falsifying physical attendance, Vela builds interactive **pre-lab simulators** where students observe apparatus, adjust controls, take realistic instrument readings with experimental uncertainty, and plot data:

### 1. Instrument Reading Bench (`instrument-bench`)
* **Vernier Caliper**: Main scale (mm) and sliding vernier scale (0.02 mm / 0.05 mm resolution). Zero-error adjustment (positive and negative zero error).
* **Micrometer Screw Gauge**: Main barrel scale (0.5 mm) and rotating thimble (50 divisions, 0.01 mm resolution). Ratchet clicking simulation.
* **Traveling Microscope**: Vertical and horizontal vernier scales for capillary tubes or lens curvature.

### 2. Linear Regression & Data Studio (`regression-studio`)
* Interactive tabular data grid with automatic uncertainty columns ($x \pm \Delta x, y \pm \Delta y$).
* Best-fit line computation ($y = mx + c$) with gradient uncertainty $\Delta m$, intercept uncertainty $\Delta c$, and Pearson correlation coefficient $r^2$.
* Dynamic error-bar rendering and residual distribution view.

### 3. Mechanics Simulator Suite
* **Air Track Bench (`air-track-sandbox`)**: Linear track with adjustable leveling tilt, dual photogates (displaying gate times $\Delta t_1, \Delta t_2$ to milliseconds), glider mass selector, and collision bumpers (spring vs velcro for elastic vs inelastic collisions).
* **Simple Pendulum Bench (`pendulum-sandbox`)**: Bob on string with length slider ($0.2\text{ m} \le L \le 1.5\text{ m}$), release angle protractor (enforcing $\theta < 10^\circ$ for small-angle approximation), and multi-oscillation digital stopwatch ($20T$ timing to minimize reaction-time error).
* **Circular Motion Whirler (`circular-motion-sandbox`)**: Rotating arm with tachometer (RPM / rad/s), radius marker $r$, and spring-loaded centripetal force scale.

### 4. Electricity & Magnetism Bench
* **Ohm's Law & Internal Resistance Workbench (`circuit-bench`)**: DC power supply, sliding wire rheostat, analog/digital ammeter and voltmeter. Students wire meters correctly (voltmeter in parallel, ammeter in series) and observe terminal voltage drop $V = \mathcal{E} - Ir$.
* **Slide-Wire Potentiometer & Meter Bridge (`meter-bridge-sandbox`)**: 1-meter resistance wire on wooden board with meter rule, sliding contact jockey, center-zero galvanometer, standard resistance box, and unknown wire sample. Real-time galvanometer needle deflection until null-point balance $L_1$ is reached.
* **Current Balance Bench (`current-balance-sandbox`)**: Parallel conductor rods with knife-edge counterweight balance, variable DC supply (0–10 A), and fractional gram weights.

### 5. Optics & Modern Physics Simulator Suite
* **Diffraction Grating Spectrometer (`spectrometer-sandbox`)**: Circular spectrometer stage with collimator, diffraction grating holder, and movable telescope with vernier angular scale. Slit image color spectrum (mercury or sodium doublet) with angular crosshair alignment.
* **Photoelectric Effect Bench (`photoelectric-sandbox`)**: Vacuum phototube illuminated by selectable monochromatic spectral filters ($365\text{ nm} \dots 577\text{ nm}$), reversing polarity voltage potentiometer, and picoammeter. Students determine stopping potential $V_0$ at zero photocurrent.
* **Thomson $e/m$ Tube Bench (`charge-mass-sandbox`)**: Fine-beam tube filled with low-pressure hydrogen gas glowing under electron impact, enclosed in paired Helmholtz coils. Current slider $I_{\text{coils}}$ changes magnetic field $B$; accelerating voltage slider $V_a$ changes electron speed; students measure glowing circular beam radius $r$ against internal mirror scale.
* **Electron Diffraction Tube Bench (`electron-diffraction-sandbox`)**: Teltron tube with graphite foil target, high-voltage HT supply (2–5 kV), and luminescent phosphor screen displaying concentric green diffraction rings ($D_1, D_2$).

## 3. Scientific Lab Report Authoring Engine

To prepare students for the 30% written report requirement and Assignment 3:
* **Structured IMRAD Template**:
  1. *Title & Student Declaration* (plagiarism statement per Section 10)
  2. *Aim / Objective* (succinct statement of physical quantity measured)
  3. *Theoretical Background* (governing equations, definitions, working formula)
  4. *Apparatus & Circuit/Setup Diagram* (labeled vector diagram)
  5. *Experimental Procedure & Precautions* (steps to minimize parallax, zero error, heating effects)
  6. *Tabulated Data & Observations* (raw data with instrument uncertainties and correct units)
  7. *Calculations, Graphs & Uncertainty Propagation* (linear regression slopes, percentage error comparison with accepted literature values)
  8. *Discussion of Errors* (systematic vs. random errors)
  9. *Conclusion* (final physical result reported as $\text{Value} \pm \Delta \text{Value}$ with correct units)

## 4. Historical assessment alignment

The weights and attendance rules below describe the supplied 2024 letter. Topic allocations are Vela preparation themes where not explicitly established by the letter. Use current official materials before giving students assessment instructions.

* **Phase 1: Online Continuous Assessment (Assignments 1–3, 20%)**:
  - Assignment 1 (6.6%): Measurements, Vernier/Micrometer readings, and error propagation.
  - Assignment 2 (6.6%): Result interpretation, significance testing, and error bounds.
  - Assignment 3 (6.8%): Graph plotting, linear regression, and formal report drafting.
  - *Must achieve $\ge 33\%$ average for admission to the Florida Campus laboratory.*
* **Phase 2: Florida Campus Laboratory Practicals (80%)**:
  - Pre-tests before each lab (10%): Tested on apparatus safety, theoretical formula, and reading procedure.
  - Practical Reports (30%): Conducted during campus lab week.
  - Post-test (40%): Summative practical examination on experimental physics.
