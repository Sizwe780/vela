# 90104–PSM · Communicative-language options

UNISA calls for one Group B module. Choose a language other than the learner’s Home or First Additional Language. Course readiness and studio features are tracked separately for each code. The speech rows propose a format, not an official lesson order. Obtain the guide and a qualified language reviewer for each code. Author dialogues independently for each language after mapping its outcomes. Follow the [communicative-language extension](../../specs/communicative-language-spec.md). For SASL, include facial expressions, signing space and movement in the recording, with suitable accessible captions or notes and a Deaf/SASL-informed reviewer. [UNISA SNL1507 module](https://www.unisa.ac.za/sites/corporate/default/Register-to-study-through-Unisa/Subjects-%26-modules/All-modules/Basic-Sign-Language-%E2%80%93-SNL1507)
