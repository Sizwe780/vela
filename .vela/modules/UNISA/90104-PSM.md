# UNISA 90104–PSM first-level curriculum

Verified against UNISA’s 90104 Physical Science and Mathematics stream on 20 November 2026. The degree’s exit level is NQF 7, SAQA 103108. The first-level structure is ten compulsory modules and one language module, totalling 132 credits. The stream table identifies curriculum level. Follow UNISA's separate registration and annual credit rules for how to schedule it. Choose one of the [eleven communicative-language options](communicative-language.md). Choose a language other than the learner’s Home or First Additional Language. The public stream page records a transition from SPE1507 to NSO1507 for students with prior credit. Vela shows the rule and does not adjudicate formal equivalence or collect a learner's home language.

PHY1501 is the legacy Vela entry. The synced catalogue archives its 90104–PSM link for new enrolments and retains student records and access where already enrolled. Mechanical lessons intended for the stream belong under PHY1505 after review. PYC1511 and PYC1512 are UNISA psychology modules outside the verified PSM first-level list. The stream lists PED3701 at the next level. The [catalogue definition](../../../lib/curriculum/psm-first-level.ts) drives the seed and grouped picker. All 21 options are present. Modules without published sessions show **Content in preparation** and can be added to a Vela plan. The initial rollout uses `node --require ./scripts/windows-runtime.cjs --import tsx prisma/seed.ts --open-first-level-placeholders` to open existing starter links. Routine seeding preserves administrative closures and archives.

Vela applies 120 credits per academic year and 60 credits for semester modules in each semester. Year modules are charged to the annual total. Learners may plan the current or next year and add their Group B choice later. Vela requests a language eligibility confirmation without asking the learner to name their Home or First Additional Languages. Vela checks same-year co-requisites against the account’s plan and lets completed Vela enrolments satisfy a co-requisite for planning. Older Vela records use the South African date of enrolment when their original plan period is missing. Formal passes, substitutions, exemptions, registration windows and placement remain managed by UNISA. [UNISA 2026 student rules, sections 9.2 and 9.4](https://m.unisa.ac.za/static/myunisa/Content/Learner%20support%20%26%20regions/Documents/Unisa%20Student%20Rules_2026.pdf)
